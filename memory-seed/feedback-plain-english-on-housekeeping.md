Report housekeeping in the owner's language. Say what was found and what it means for them ("two pages link to a page that no longer exists; I have pointed them at the right one"), not the mechanism ("G6 fired on two dangling wikilinks").

**Why:** the owner supervises the vault through conversation. A report they cannot follow is a report they cannot act on, and it hands the decision to the assistant by default.

**How to apply:** translate every finding into what it means and what, if anything, the owner needs to decide. Name a file only when the owner has to go there. If the mechanism matters, explain it in one sentence; otherwise leave it out.
