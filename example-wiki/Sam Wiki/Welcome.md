# Welcome

**Sam is invented, and this is an example wiki to look at.** Nothing in it belongs to a real person. It is here so you can see a finished wiki before you start your own, because "what is a wiki?" is a question that is much easier to answer by showing than by explaining.

Read it the way Sam would: open a page, follow a link, come back. There are six pages worth reading and it takes under ten minutes.

## Where to start

Open [[Bread]] first. It is the page that shows what a wiki is for. Sam did not sit down and write it. It was built up over four months of Saturday conversations, and you can see the dates where each piece arrived. Something Sam mentioned in passing in May turned out to explain a failed loaf in September, and the page had kept it.

Then look at the other two pages Sam has: [[Bicycle]] and [[Reading]].

## The four files that run it

[[Index]] is the catalogue. One line per page, so you can see everything Sam has.

[[_context]] is the working state. What Sam is in the middle of, and what the assistant is waiting on an answer about. There is an open question on it, because there is always an open question on a real one.

[[log]] is the chronology. Every session, dated, in the order it happened. The gaps are real: Sam did nothing at all in August.

`CLAUDE.md`, in this folder, is the rules file. Sam's assistant reads it at the start of every session so it knows how Sam likes things written.

## What Sam never does

Sam does not open these files to write in them. Sam talks, in ordinary words, and the assistant writes the page, adds the log entry and saves the change. Every page here arrived that way.
