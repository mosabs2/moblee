# Reading

Sam borrows paperbacks from the library, three or four at a time, and gives them back. Titles are usually gone by the time Sam mentions the book, so what this page records is the subject and Sam's verdict in Sam's own words. Anything Sam does not say is left off the page rather than guessed at.

## 2026

**A book about baking, borrowed in late June 2026.** Bought Sam round to feeding the starter by weight rather than by the spoonful, which is the note dated 4 July 2026 on [[Bread]]. Sam's verdict: useful for the numbers and far too pleased with itself about the rest.

**A history of the local canals, borrowed in July 2026.** Sam read about forty pages and stopped. Too many dates and no people in it.

**A novel about a lighthouse keeper, borrowed in July 2026.** Sam finished it in three evenings and said it was the best thing read all year. No other detail was offered, so none is recorded.

## What this page is for

Sam's reason for wanting it was small and practical: standing in the library holding a book and not knowing whether it had already been read. Three entries do not solve that yet. Twenty will.
