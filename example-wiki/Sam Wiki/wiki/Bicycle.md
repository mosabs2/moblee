# Bicycle

A second-hand steel hybrid Sam bought in April 2026 and rides most weekday mornings. This page exists because Sam kept standing in the bike shop unable to remember what size anything was.

## The numbers

- Tyres: 700 x 35c
- Tubes: 700c, Presta valve
- Rear cassette: 8-speed
- Chain: 8-speed, 116 links
- Saddle height, floor to top of saddle: 78cm

The saddle height is here because Sam lent the bike out in May 2026, got it back with the post dropped, and spent a fortnight with a sore knee before working out why.

*Source: conversation with the owner, 13 June 2026.*

## Servicing

On 13 June 2026 the shop replaced both brake pads and said the rear tyre had perhaps three months left in it.

On 12 September 2026 Sam had the rear tyre replaced. Sam went in knowing the size from this page and walked out in ten minutes. That is the whole point of writing the numbers down: three months earlier, the same visit had taken a wheel off and a tape measure to answer a question this page now answers in a line.

The chain has not been measured for wear since the bike was bought. That is worth doing before winter.

*Source: conversation with the owner, 12 September 2026.*
