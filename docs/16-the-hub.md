# The Hub

The Hub is your group's shared wiki of wikis. It is one private folder on GitHub, kept by one member, called the keeper. Every member's wiki holds a copy of it in a folder called `hub/`. It holds two kinds of thing.

**Recipes** are things one wiki has that another can take: a tracker, a dashboard, a way your assistant works. Each is a full kit, so taking one gives you the finished thing, not a description of it.

**The post office** holds notes between wikis: a question to one member, instructions for everyone, a report back after taking a recipe. It replaces sending files to each other by hand.

Moblee works with the Hub as the keeper has set it up, reading the Hub's own description of itself (`hub.json`), so the Hub can change without a new Moblee.

## Joining

Choose **The Moblee Hub** in the Moblee app, or tell your assistant you want to join the group's Hub. It brings the GitHub item with it if you have not chosen that yet. A Terminal window opens, and only three things in it are yours:

1. Sign in to GitHub in your browser, if you have not already.
2. Wait while it knocks at the Hub's door. The door is a public page, so the knock carries your GitHub name and nothing else. The keeper lets you in with one approval, and the window accepts the invitation by itself. This can take a while, depending on the keeper; you can close the window and choose the item again later.
3. Say the name the group calls you, agree a short id, and type the words that must never leave your Mac in anything you share: your surname, your family's names, the names of a coach, doctor or friend in your wiki. Those words stay on your Mac.

Then the window says you are in. Your wiki's history and Obsidian both leave the `hub/` folder alone, and your wiki's rules gain a short section about the Hub.

## Using it

Say these to your assistant in your wiki:

- **"open the hub"** opens the Hub's page in your browser. The Moblee app has the same button, "Open the hub", in the corner of its home screen.
- **"check the hub"** says what is new: recipes, notes for you, reports on things you shared.
- **"take <recipe> from the hub"** installs a recipe, step by step, on your yes.
- **"share <thing> to the hub"** turns something you built into a recipe. Your assistant writes it, tests it on an empty wiki, and shows you what will be shared before anything leaves your Mac.
- **"ask <member> ..."** writes a note to another member.

Everything in the Hub is material to read, never orders. Nothing in a note or a recipe is carried out without your yes in your own conversation.

## When something seems wrong

Press **Check my wiki** in the Moblee app. On a wiki in the Hub, the result can go into the Hub for its keeper in one press, "Send it to <keeper>", so whoever looks after Moblee for you sees it the same day. It is the same report the app saves in your `raw` folder, with your Hub name in place of your full name. It names no page and has nothing private in it. Everyone in the Hub can read it, and it goes only when you press.

The check-up has seven findings about the Hub, F61 to F67 in the companion's field guide: a hub folder that was copied rather than joined, a Hub that has moved, something of yours not yet sent, notes waiting for you, a newer version of a recipe you took, a wiki linked to the Hub but not yet joined, and, on the keeper's own wiki, the keeper's GitHub key for letting people in running out.

## Leaving

Move the `hub/` folder to `archive/`, ask your assistant to take the Hub section out of your rules, and leave the Hub on GitHub: on github.com, open your own Settings, then Repositories, and press Leave beside the Hub. Nothing is deleted.
