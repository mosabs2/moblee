Where these samples came from

Each file here is what `python3 scripts/moblee-doctor.py --prove-guard --json`
prints: a JSON list of rows, each with "level", "text" and "guide", as
`json.dumps(f.rows, indent=2)` writes them.

They were NOT captured from a run. They were put together by hand on
20 September 2026 from the script's own source (scripts/moblee-doctor.py at
branch second-assistant): the level words from its constants (OK, PROBLEM,
CANNOT TELL), the sentences copied from the f.add(...) calls in prove_guard,
check_codex_guard, check_chatgpt, check_diary and main, with TRUST_CHECK and
TRUST_FIX written out in full, and the guide codes as those calls give them.
The rows a real run adds about the Mac, the wiki's pages and the scheduled
jobs are left out; the app reads none of them. The scratch folder's date is
made up.

timed-out.json was added on 25 September 2026 in the same way, from the
prove_guard timeout line, with TRUST_CHECK written out in full and a made-up
scratch folder date. It is the run that overstays the three-minute limit: the
check-up says so at CANNOT TELL, having deleted nothing, and the app must read
that as "could not tell" rather than as a guard that is not running.
tools/test-doctor.py checks that the sentence in it is still the sentence the
check-up says.

The app's logic check (`--check-logic --fixtures <this folder's parent>`)
reads them to make sure the app still knows a proved guard, a guard that is
not running, and "could not tell" when it sees the check-up's real words.

Replace them with captured output after the first real proof on the test
account, and say so here. If the check-up's wording or its guide codes change,
change these in the same commit, or the logic check will pass on words the
check-up no longer says.
